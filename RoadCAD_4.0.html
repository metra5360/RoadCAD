Road Parameters

Tram Parameters

AI Routes

Draw the trajectory along which the AI traffic will move. The line can pass along the road and through intersections.

🌉 Bridges (B)

Works like roads: Ground point → Bridge point = entrance (cars climb the ramp). Bridge → Bridge = on the bridge. Bridge → Ground = exit. Ground-level routes pass under bridges; cars drive over a ramp or bridge only on Bridge-level points. Bridge parts of a route are highlighted in blue.

Left Click — route points · Enter/Double Click — finalize · Esc — cancel

🚗 AI-traffic

Cars use only AI routes, follow traffic lights, distances and random speed.

Lights
